# Compliance basics (not legal advice — no Legal Bot; consider counsel)
- **TCPA:** marketing texts sent with an autodialer/platform need prior express written consent. Penalties $500–$1,500 per message. 1,049 x 3 texts = large exposure if consent fails.
- **2018 consent risk (biggest risk):** consent is ~8 years old, and numbers get reassigned. Check numbers against the FCC Reassigned Numbers Database before sending, and keep proof of how/when each person opted in. If proof doesn't exist, consider sending only Message 1 as a re-permission ask, or don't send.
- **DNC:** scrub against the National Do Not Call Registry and any internal suppression list.
- **CTIA:** identify sender in every message (done: "Infinitum Corporation"), include opt-out ("Reply STOP to opt out"), support HELP, honor STOP/UNSUBSCRIBE/CANCEL/END/QUIT immediately (FCC 2025 rules: honor any reasonable opt-out within 10 business days, one confirmation text allowed). No SHAFT content.
- **Quiet hours:** TCPA allows 8am–9pm *recipient local time*; some states are stricter (e.g. FL, OK: 8am–8pm and max 3 msgs/24h). List is ~all Eastern (704/980 Charlotte NC 728; 803/864/843 SC; 336 NC). Send window 12:00–16:00 ET (= 20:00–00:00 MSK) keeps everyone safe incl. any Central/Mountain/Pacific.
- **Opt-out handling:** provider auto STOP list; export opt-outs after each wave and suppress in later waves; never re-text an opted-out number.
- Keep records: list source, consent proof, send logs, opt-outs, 4+ years.
