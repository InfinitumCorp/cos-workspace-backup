- Abstract:
  Corporate Cognitive Warfare, a term coined by William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum Corporation, is the strategic use of irregular warfare principles to shape how customers, employees, investors, regulators and the public perceive and decide, and to defend an organization against hostile influence. This expanded edition grounds the concept in a neutral review of published military literature: Russian reflexive control theory, Chinese cognitive domain operations and the "three warfares," and NATO research on cognitive warfare.
