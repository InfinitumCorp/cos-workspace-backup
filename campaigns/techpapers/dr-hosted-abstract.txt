This whitepaper examines where artificial intelligence helps a security team, where it hurts, and how a mid-size organization can adopt it without losing control. It reads the problem through the lens of irregular warfare, the study of competition below the threshold of open war by indirect, deniable and asymmetric means, and draws general lessons for defenders from public analysis of the modern battlefield, where constant observation and jammed or spoofed signals make any single signal unreliable. The paper is strictly analytical and defensive and takes no side regarding any party. It reviews the evidence on AI for defenders and analyzes six security operations use cases: alert triage, threat-intelligence enrichment, vulnerability prioritization, log analysis, reporting, and deception and early warning. It then covers governance under the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, securing AI systems themselves, people and the workforce, metrics, and hypothetical illustrative scenarios. Its central recommendation is to govern first, measure a baseline, pilot with analysts in the loop, point AI at exposure, secure the AI itself, and widen automation only as evidence supports it. Statistics are drawn from cited public sources and are not benchmarks or guarantees for any organization.

Author: William Aaron Turner, Founder & President, Infinitum Corporation®, Washington, DC. Whitepaper No. 1, released October 25, 2026.
