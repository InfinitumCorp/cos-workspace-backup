Section 04

Founder: William Aaron Turner

William Aaron Turner is the founder and president of Infinitum Corporation, a technology and security consulting firm headquartered in Washington, DC.

According to the company’s August 2026 announcement, Turner “focuses on the integration of emerging technologies with contemporary defense and security concepts,” and has “spent years developing technical expertise in cybersecurity, secure communications, network defense, and emerging technologies.”

Turner writes the firm’s public research: the Infinitum Corporation Whitepapers (Dead Reckoning: AI for Security Operations; Russia’s State-Influenced Capitalism), The Ukraine Files series, and the Russia Africa Report. In his own words: “I’m not a journalist. I’m a businessman.”
