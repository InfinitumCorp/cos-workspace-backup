# References check

Manuscript: *Adversary-aware evaluation of large language model assistants for security alert triage* (Technical Note).

All 34 references were checked on 31 Oct 2026 (Moscow time, UTC+3) against the publisher, the standards body, or the arXiv record (title, authors, year, venue, DOI/URL). Methods: Crossref API, DOI content negotiation, USENIX/PMLR/JMLR/NeurIPS citation meta tags, the arXiv API, NIST PDFs via doi.org, and the live OWASP, MITRE, and ENISA pages. One candidate source was dropped because I could not check the claim I meant to attribute to it (see the end of this file).

Every number in the manuscript text comes from a cited source: '30 papers' and 'ten pitfalls' [Arp et al.]; '88.2%', 'three production LLMs', '90.4%', '8.4%' [Karanjai et al.]. All other numerals are section, step, dimension, or identifier labels (e.g., AML.T0051, LLM01, CSF 2.0).

- Karanjai et al. (USENIX Security 2026) has no page numbers in the proceedings PDF yet; the proceedings URL is given. The arXiv version is 2607.14493.
