I submit the research article **"State-Guided Adaptation: The Resilience of the Russian Economy under the 2022–2025 Sanctions Regime"** («Государственно направляемая адаптация: устойчивость российской экономики в условиях санкционного режима 2022–2025 гг.») for consideration in *MGIMO Review of International Relations*.

The article asks which institutional features of Russia's political economy explain the gap between the forecasts of economic collapse made in spring 2022 and the outcome. Using a comparison of forecasts with outcomes, process tracing and macroeconomic data, it identifies four mechanisms of state-guided adaptation (fiscal discipline and low debt, monetary and financial sovereignty, state control of strategic sectors, and the reorientation of trade and payments) and the costs of that adaptation. It engages with the Russian scholarship on sanctions effectiveness published in the journal, including the work of V.A. Silaeva, O.I. Rebro, A.S. Lebedev and Yu.V. Borovsky, and with the international literature on economic statecraft.
