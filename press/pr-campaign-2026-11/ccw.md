# Corporate Cognitive Warfare Expanded Edition: William Aaron Turner Releases 15,000-Word Paper on Competing for Perception Lawfully

Infinitum Corporation releases the expanded edition of Corporate Cognitive Warfare, the discipline coined by William Aaron Turner, now free on Academia.edu and Zenodo.

WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026 - Infinitum Corporation today announced the expanded edition of "Corporate Cognitive Warfare: Irregular Warfare for Market Supremacy," a free white paper by William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum Corporation, who coined the term Corporate Cognitive Warfare.

The expanded edition grows the original paper to about 15,000 words across 38 pages. It keeps the original definitions and the six-phase framework of Understand, Shape, Seed, Amplify, Exploit and Protect, and adds four new bodies of material for executives, communicators and security leaders.

First, a neutral doctrinal foundation. The paper reviews published research on Russian reflexive control theory, Chinese cognitive domain operations and the "three warfares," and NATO's work on cognitive warfare, drawing on sources from the Foreign Military Studies Office, the Naval Postgraduate School, RAND, the China Aerospace Studies Institute and the U.S. Department of Defense. Each tradition is presented analytically, with the caveats its own analysts raise.

Second, detailed corporate applications. Guerrilla marketing, ethical persuasion psychology, algorithmic optimization, social influence and targeted advertising are each placed inside the rules that govern them, including FTC endorsement rules, GDPR, the EU Digital Services Act, CCPA/CPRA and platform policies.

Third, twelve illustrative use cases, ranging from a challenger brand reframing its category to countering a viral falsehood, defending against a fake-review attack, responding to an executive deepfake and staying visible in AI-generated answers.

Fourth, a defensive playbook: a threat taxonomy, the DISARM shared vocabulary, narrative sensing, prebunking, decision hygiene against provocation, response tiers and regular exercises.

The paper draws a firm line. Fake accounts, bot networks, astroturfing, fabricated reviews and disinformation are treated only as threats to detect and defeat, never as tools.

The white paper is published under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license with the DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23838097.
