# William Aaron Turner Publishes Analysis of the Lobbying, Charity and Media Networks Around Ukraine's Government

**Summary:** Part 6 of William Aaron Turner's Ukraine Files maps FARA lobbying disclosures, large charity procurement and media relationships connected to Ukraine's government since 2019.

**Dateline:** WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026

William Aaron Turner, founder and president of Infinitum Corporation, has published "Zelenskyy's Rise: Backers, Media and Political Network," Part 6 of his research series The Ukraine Files. The article maps the intelligence, lobbying, charity and media relationships around Volodymyr Zelenskyy's government, drawing on public reporting and disclosures.

What the article maps

- Lobbying: a Quincy Institute analysis finding that 46 firms or individuals registered under FARA in 2022-2023 to represent Ukrainian interests, reporting more than 12,000 political activities.
- Charities: published procurement figures for large Ukrainian foundations, including Come Back Alive and the Serhiy Prytula Foundation, and their delivery of drones and satellite capability.
- Intelligence cooperation: New York Times reporting on CIA cooperation with Ukrainian intelligence since 2014-2016.
- Media: public statements by a former presidential press secretary.

Sourcing and labeling

The article says that everything in it is either the author's argument or a claim attributed to a named source. It reports allegations by named commentators as allegations, not as established facts, and records official dismissals and denials where they exist. A numbered source list, including the Quincy Institute, The Atlantic, Militarnyi, Defense Post and Sky News, accompanies the article.

"The article follows public disclosures: lobbying filings, charity procurement and published reporting. Readers can check each source." said William Aaron Turner, Founder & President, Infinitum Corporation.
