# William Aaron Turner Publishes Commentary on the History and Human-Rights Record of Ukraine's Azov Unit

**Summary:** An opinion commentary by William Aaron Turner reviews the origins of Ukraine's Azov unit, its integration into the National Guard, and findings by the UN OHCHR and Human Rights Watch.

**Dateline:** WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026

William Aaron Turner, founder and president of Infinitum Corporation, has published an opinion commentary on Medium about the history of Ukraine's Azov unit, from its formation as a volunteer battalion in 2014 to its current status as a brigade of the National Guard.

What the commentary covers

- Origins: the unit's founding in 2014, its founder's political background, and the founding of the National Corps party in 2016.
- Integration: entry into the National Guard in November 2014 and reorganization as the 12th Special Forces Brigade in 2023.
- U.S. policy: the 2018 congressional restriction on U.S. assistance to the unit and the State Department's decision to lift it in June 2024.
- Human-rights record: allegations documented by the UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights in 2016, and by Human Rights Watch and Amnesty International.
- Rebranding: the unit's reported change of insignia in 2022, which the author discusses in the context of academic research on extremism.

Framing

The piece is labeled as opinion and draws a historical comparison with U.S. extremist movements. Claims from Russian authorities are attributed to them. The bibliography includes Stanford's Mapping Militants Project, the UN OHCHR, Human Rights Watch and the Equal Justice Initiative.

"The commentary follows the unit's documented history and the human-rights findings, with each source named." said William Aaron Turner, Founder & President, Infinitum Corporation.
