# Fifty Countries, One Russia: William Aaron Turner's X Article The Art of Not Giving a F*ck Tallies the Western Coalition's Results

William Aaron Turner's satirical long-form X Article examines the 50-plus-nation Ukraine Defense Contact Group, Western aid totals and the state of the war.

WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026 - William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum Corporation, is promoting his long-form X Article "The Art of Not Giving a F*ck: How Russia Is Laughing All the Way to Victory While the West Panics," a satirical analysis of the Western coalition supporting Ukraine.

Written in an openly sardonic voice, the article sets one country against a coalition. It notes that the Ukraine Defense Contact Group, the Ramstein-format coordination mechanism, includes more than fifty countries, and asks why that combined military, economic and political weight has produced what Turner describes as a stalemate that increasingly favors Moscow.

The article works through the aid numbers country by country. It notes that the United States was historically the largest military donor before the administration halted direct funding and pressed Europe to take the lead. It reports that Germany became the leading European military donor in 2026 with €11.5 billion allocated, and roughly €39 billion in humanitarian aid and €55 billion in military assistance since February 2022. It lists the United Kingdom as the third-largest military aid provider in 2026, with $18.45 billion.

Turner then sets those totals against the situation on the ground, noting that Russia controls about a fifth of Ukraine more than four years into the war, and against the cost to Western economies and budgets. The conclusions are presented as the author's argument and are meant to provoke debate among readers who follow the war, sanctions and European defense spending.

The piece is part of Turner's wider body of work on sanctions and Russia's economy, which includes Special Edition 01 of the Infinitum whitepaper series, "Russia's State-Influenced Capitalism." Turner coined the term Corporate Cognitive Warfare, the discipline of competing for perception lawfully and defending against narrative attacks.
