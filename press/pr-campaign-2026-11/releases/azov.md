# Azov Movement: William Aaron Turner's Article Examines the Unit's Founding, Symbols and Human Rights Record

William Aaron Turner's Medium article compiles reporting and research on the Azov movement's origins, symbols and the abuse allegations documented by the UN and rights groups.

WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026 - William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum Corporation, has published a long-form opinion article on Medium examining Ukraine's Azov movement, its founding, its symbols and its documented human rights record.

The article is written as an argued comparison: Turner contends that Azov's ideology and imagery invite comparison with Nazi Germany and the Ku Klux Klan, and he says plainly that the piece is meant to make readers uncomfortable. The conclusions are his; the facts are attributed to the sources he cites.

On origins, the article reports that the unit was founded in 2014 by Andriy Biletsky, previously the founder of the group Patriot of Ukraine, and traces its intellectual roots to Dmytro Dontsov's "integral nationalism." It describes Biletsky's ties to Arsen Avakov, then interior minister, and the support of oligarch Ihor Kolomoisky.

On symbols, the article details the unit's use of the Wolfsangel and the Black Sun, notes that the Anti-Defamation League lists the Wolfsangel and the Totenkopf as hate symbols, cites scholarly research identifying hundreds of cases of Nazi and neo-Nazi symbol displays by movement members, and records that Azov reportedly dropped the Wolfsangel insignia in 2022, a change Turner treats as a rebranding.

On conduct, the article cites a 2016 report by the UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights that documented allegations of looting, arbitrary detention and ill-treatment by members of Azov and other volunteer battalions, as well as allegations raised by Human Rights Watch and Amnesty International. It also sets out Russian court convictions and Russian accounts, identified as such.

Turner argues that Western governments and media should look at this record directly before describing the movement to their audiences. Readers are encouraged to check each source. Turner coined the term Corporate Cognitive Warfare, the discipline of competing for perception lawfully and defending against narrative attacks.
