# Ukraine Aid NGOs: William Aaron Turner's Ukraine Files Opens With a Call to Audit Where Charity Donations Go

Part 1 of The Ukraine Files by William Aaron Turner asks donors to follow the trail from consumer charity websites to military and intelligence work.

WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026 - William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum Corporation, has published "Ukraine Aid NGOs: How to Audit Where Donations Go," the opening file of his eight-part series The Ukraine Files.

The file is built around one question for anyone who gives through a charity website: where does the donation end up? Turner argues that some Western NGOs with consumer-friendly websites and card donations are connected to military and intelligence work, and that the donation and aid trail deserves an independent audit. Those conclusions are presented as Turner's own argument.

The post serves as the entry point to the series' NGO thread and points readers to three companion files. "Zelenskyy's Rise: Backers, Media and Political Network" covers charities and drone-funding groups including Come Back Alive, the Serhiy Prytula Foundation, Wild Hornets and Razom for Ukraine, along with public FARA lobbying filings. "Demining NGOs and Intelligence: The Laos and Moldova Claims" examines the HALO Trust's funding and board and the espionage allegations collected in Turner's report "Sweeps and Spies." "In-Q-Tel Explained" follows the CIA's venture capital arm into the defense-technology pipeline.

The Ukraine Files covers the money, the NGOs, the intelligence networks and the outcomes of the war in Ukraine. Each post links to a long-form original with its reference list, attributes claims to named people, outlets or governments, and labels conclusions as the author's own. Readers are encouraged to check each source and reach their own view.

For donors, the practical takeaway is a checklist mindset: read an organization's filings, check who sits on its board, see who funds it and ask what the money is actually used for before giving.

Turner is also the author of the Infinitum Corporation whitepaper series. Turner coined the term Corporate Cognitive Warfare, the discipline of competing for perception lawfully and defending against narrative attacks.
