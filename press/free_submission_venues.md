# Free places for William Aaron Turner / Infinitum Corp to submit articles
Researched Sat 31 Oct 2026 (Moscow, UTC+3). Nothing was contacted or submitted.
"Free" means there's no fee to submit or publish. None of the outlets in group (a) pay contributors, except where a note says otherwise.
URL check: every URL below returned HTTP 200 today, except those marked (403). Those sites block automated checks; the pages exist in search indexes and should open in a normal browser.

## Read first
1. **US sanctions risk.** OFAC designated **Strategic Culture Foundation** on 15 Apr 2021 (E.O. 13848) and **New Eastern Outlook** on 3 Mar 2022 (E.O. 14024). Sources: treasury.gov press releases jy0126 and jy0628. William is a US citizen, so providing services to designated entities may carry legal exposure. That's a lawyer question; I'm not giving a legal opinion. InfoBRICS was not found on OFAC lists in this search.
2. **Exclusivity.** His pieces are already on his blogs. Many venues want unpublished work. The venues that openly accept reprints or don't demand exclusivity are marked "REPRINT OK" or "no exclusivity stated".
3. **AI rule.** Modern Diplomacy and The Libertarian Institute explicitly refuse AI-generated or AI-assisted content. SocArXiv bans AI-produced papers that lack original content.
4. **Academia AI and Applications.** The AI-security paper is under review there. Don't post that paper to a preprint server or another journal until that journal's preprint policy has been checked.

## Top 5 picks
1. **Global Research**: high-traffic alternative geopolitics outlet. Sends news and opinion articles in the email body, with no exclusivity rule stated.
2. **Antiwar.com**: big anti-interventionist audience. Prefers originals but reprints exceptional pieces. Good for Ukraine Files.
3. **Countercurrents**: easy email submission, no exclusivity stated, under 1,000 words. Good for the Russia-Africa and Global South pieces.
4. **Eurasia Review**: large geopolitics and security site. Requires original work, so use it for NEW pieces (pitch first). Also fits AI and cyber op-eds.
5. **SocArXiv (OSF) + Zenodo**: free DOIs for whitepapers and long analyses, and they accept already-shared work. SocArXiv requires a public ORCID linked to the OSF profile; he now has ORCID 0009-0002-1585-5442.
