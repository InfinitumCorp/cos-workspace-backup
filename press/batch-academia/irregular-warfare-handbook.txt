The Irregular Warfare Handbook by William Aaron Turner: operating, influencing and defending in the information environment, within the law of armed conflict.

BODY:
WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026 - Infinitum Corporation today announced that William Aaron Turner, Founder & President, has published "The Irregular Warfare Handbook: Cognitive Engagement for the Modern Warfighter," a field handbook on operating, influencing and defending within the information environment. It explains irregular warfare and why the information environment is decisive, the four core functions of information engagement, offensive influence ("Your Post Is a Munition," the influence matrix and team-based amplification), defensive cognitive survivability (digital signature, recognizing hostile cognitive attacks and personal information hygiene), and rules of engagement: online, the law of armed conflict still applies.

"Every warfighter now operates in the information environment, whether they know it or not," said William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum Corporation.
