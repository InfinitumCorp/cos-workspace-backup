Decentralized Disinformation: William Aaron Turner's Study of How Organic Narratives Are Weaponized Online

SUMMARY:
Decentralized Disinformation by William Aaron Turner: the actor-affect-affordance model, cross-platform amplification, content shadowing and breadcrumbing.

BODY:
WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026 - Infinitum Corporation today announced that William Aaron Turner, Founder & President, has published "Decentralized Disinformation: The Art of Weaponizing Organic Narratives," a research study on the new face of disinformation. It explains what makes disinformation "decentralized," the Actor-Affect-Affordance model and the multiplier effect, then the four-step mechanics of amplification: identifying seed content, cross-platform repackaging, narrative evolution and coordinated amplification. A chapter on "content shadowing" examines the blending of truth and falsehood, why it works, examples in practice, and the "breadcrumbing" technique.

"The most effective disinformation doesn't look manufactured. It looks organic, and that is the point," said William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum Corporation.
