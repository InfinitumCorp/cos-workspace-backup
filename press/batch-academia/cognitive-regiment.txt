HEADLINE:
The Cognitive Regiment: William Aaron Turner Releases a Regimental-Level Psychological Operations Handbook

SUMMARY:
The Cognitive Regiment by William Aaron Turner: an open-source handbook on regimental PSYOP structure, targeting, propaganda types and digital operations.

BODY:
WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026 - Infinitum Corporation today announced that William Aaron Turner, Founder & President, has published "The Cognitive Regiment: A Modern Tactical Handbook for Psychological Operations at Regimental Level," an unclassified, open-source reference. Part I covers the cognitive battlespace and regimental PSYOP structure; Part II the targeting cycle and white, gray and black propaganda; Part III leaflet, loudspeaker, digital and social media, unmanned-systems and cyber-enabled operations; Part IV assessment and effectiveness, with field checklists and reference cards.

"The cognitive battlespace is real terrain, and regiments need doctrine for it," said William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum Corporation.
