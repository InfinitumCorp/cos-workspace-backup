HEADLINE:
Battlefield Cyber Hygiene: William Aaron Turner Publishes a Warfighter's Guide to Metadata, Signal and Phone Discipline

SUMMARY:
Battlefield Cyber Hygiene by William Aaron Turner: a digital OPSEC guide on Faraday protection, metadata, social media, geolocation and daily cyber habits.

BODY:
WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026 - Infinitum Corporation today announced that William Aaron Turner, Founder & President, has published "Battlefield Cyber Hygiene: A Modern Warfighter's Digital Survival Guide," a digital OPSEC field guide. Its premise: the modern battlefield is defined by visibility in the electromagnetic spectrum, where "a smartphone ping, a fitness tracker upload, or a social media photo can become a targeting beacon." It opens with Maj. Gen. Curtis Taylor's warning, "This device is going to get our soldiers killed," and cases including an Apache route mapped from a phone moving at 120 mph and a HIMARS strike on a barracks after unauthorized phone use.

Chapters cover Faraday technology as the first line of defense, metadata as the hidden threat, social media as an intelligence goldmine, geolocation and becoming invisible to trackers, daily cyber-hygiene discipline, and defensive cyber tools, closing on the principle that digital silence is survival.

"Your phone is a beacon. Discipline is what turns it off," said William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum Corporation.
