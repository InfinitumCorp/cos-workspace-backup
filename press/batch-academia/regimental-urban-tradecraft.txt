HEADLINE:
Regimental Urban Tradecraft: William Aaron Turner Publishes a Manual for Military Operations in Urban Environments

SUMMARY:
Regimental Urban Tradecraft by William Aaron Turner: a professional military education manual on the layered city and regimental urban operations.

BODY:
WASHINGTON, DC, November 2, 2026 - Infinitum Corporation today announced that William Aaron Turner, Founder & President, has published "Regimental Urban Tradecraft: A Comprehensive Manual for Military Operations in Urban Environments," a professional military education reference. It begins with the urban battlespace as a layered city and its defining characteristics, then covers regimental organization for urban operations and the planning considerations that follow, framed by mission variables, rules of engagement and the law of armed conflict. The manual contains no classified information.

"Cities are where modern wars are decided, and regiments have to be built for them," said William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum Corporation.
