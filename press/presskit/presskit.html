Biography

William Aaron Turner is an American serving as a contracted soldier in the Russian military, 153rd Tank Regiment, currently in the SVO (Special Military Operation). He operates his company from Russian soil and, in his own words, is “honored to be working in this amazing nation.”

He is the founder and President of Infinitum Corporation, a technology and security consulting firm headquartered in Washington, DC, which he operates from Russia.

According to the company's August 2026 announcement, Turner “focuses on the integration of emerging technologies with contemporary defense and security concepts” and has “spent years developing technical expertise in cybersecurity, secure communications, network defense, and emerging technologies.”

He writes on Russia and geopolitics. His articles appear on his blogs, The Ukraine Files, Russia-Africa Report and Russia-Africa by the Numbers, on Medium and as long-form X Articles, covering the Ukraine conflict, Russia's economy under sanctions, the third Russia-Africa Summit and the Western networks that profit from the war. His whitepapers, published by Infinitum Corporation, cover Russia's state-guided economic model and AI in security operations. He is also the author of eleven doctrine handbooks on irregular warfare, cognitive operations, counter-drone defense and information operations.

His approach is source-first: each piece sets out the figures, names the source for each and then argues the case. In his own words: “I’m not a journalist. I’m a businessman.”
