HEADLINE:
William Aaron Turner, Founder of Infinitum Corporation, Expands Free Security and Geopolitical Research Library

SUMMARY:
William Aaron Turner, founder of Washington, DC-based Infinitum Corporation, publishes free whitepapers on AI security operations and sanctions analysis.

BODY:
WASHINGTON, DC, October 31, 2026 - William Aaron Turner, founder and president of Infinitum Corporation, a Washington, DC technology and security consulting firm with Moscow as an operating location, is expanding the company's free public research library with whitepapers and long-form analysis for security leaders, executives and policy readers.

Turner works at the intersection of information technology, cybersecurity and modern security operations. Through Infinitum Corporation, he advises organizations on cybersecurity consulting, network architecture and network defense, ethical hacking and vulnerability assessment, artificial intelligence applications for security operations, and strategic risk assessment.

"As warfare and technology continue to converge, success increasingly depends upon the ability to integrate intelligence, cyber capabilities and operational decision-making into a unified framework," said Turner. "Infinitum Corporation was established to bridge traditional security disciplines with the technological realities of the modern battlefield."

Infinitum Corporation Whitepapers

Turner publishes the company's formal research on the Infinitum Corporation Whitepapers blog. Two papers are available free of charge:

Dead Reckoning: AI for Security Operations in a Jammed, Spoofed, Watched World. Whitepaper 01 asks what public analysis of jammed and spoofed signal environments teaches the people who defend organizations. It includes a 90-day plan, a 12-month roadmap and a quarterly check for security teams adopting AI. Turner's advice in the paper: "Govern first. Measure a baseline. Pilot with analysts in the loop. Keep an independent check on every critical signal."

Russia's State-Influenced Capitalism: Sanctions-Proof, War-Proof, and Chaos-Proof. Special Edition 01 compares the IMF's April 2022 forecast of an 8.5 percent contraction in Russia's economy with the IMF's current figures, and includes a full chapter on what the critics get right. It is presented as the author's argued position, with every figure attributed to its source.

The Ukraine Files

Turner also writes The Ukraine Files, an eight-part series that takes one thread at a time, covering topics such as sanctions and the Russian economy, the Eastern Economic Forum and In-Q-Tel. Each post is based on a full long-form article and links to the original with its reference list. When a claim comes from a named person, outlet or government, the post says who made it, and conclusions are labeled as Turner's own argument.

Evidence first

A common thread runs through Turner's work: readers are asked to check the sources rather than accept the conclusions on trust. "Don't take my word for it. Read the sources," he wrote when releasing the first whitepaper.

Looking ahead, Infinitum Corporation plans further research into autonomous systems, secure communications, unmanned aerial systems and advanced network resilience.
