Ukraine aid NGOs: William Aaron Turner's first Ukraine Files entry follows the chain from CIA bases to charity tax IDs and calls for a donation audit.

BODY:
WASHINGTON, DC, October 31, 2026 - Ukraine aid NGOs and where donations actually go are the subject of "Ukraine Aid NGOs: How to Audit Where Donations Go," Part 1 of 8 in The Ukraine Files by William Aaron Turner, president of Infinitum Corporation. The post introduces the opening file of the series, "Audit Ukraine Aid Now."

The claim

"Western NGOs with nice websites and credit-card donations are arming Ukrainian special forces and running satellite intelligence," the post states. Turner says the file "follows the chain from CIA bases to charity tax IDs."

What the file covers

Consumer-facing charities: How Western NGOs with consumer-friendly websites and card donations connect to military and intelligence work.

The money trail: The documented chain from CIA bases to charity tax IDs, as Turner describes it.

The case for an audit: Why Turner thinks the donation and aid trail deserves an audit.

Turner frames the file as the opening entry of the series, which follows the NGO thread through charities, lobbying, demining organizations and the CIA's venture arm.

Where the thread continues

The post is a teaser: the full article for this file has not yet been linked on the blog, and Turner says the complete write-up is on its way. In the meantime, the post points readers to the three files in the series that follow the NGO thread in detail.

Zelenskyy's Rise: Backers, Media and Political Network covers the charities and drone-funding groups Turner describes, including Come Back Alive, the Serhiy Prytula Foundation, Wild Hornets and Razom for Ukraine, plus the FARA lobbying figures.

Demining NGOs and Intelligence: The Laos and Moldova Claims covers the HALO Trust's funding and board, and the espionage allegations collected in Turner's report "Sweeps and Spies."

In-Q-Tel Explained follows the CIA's venture arm into the defense-technology pipeline.

Why it matters to donors

The file centers on one question for anyone who gives through a charity website: where does the donation end up? Turner's position, stated in the post, is that the donation and aid trail deserves an audit.
