## Title (68 chars)
Infinitum Corporation Releases AI for Security Operations Whitepaper

## Summary / meta description (153 chars)
Infinitum Corporation's first whitepaper, "Dead Reckoning," asks what jammed and spoofed battlefields teach security teams about using AI in operations.

## Body

WASHINGTON, D.C. - October 27, 2026 - Infinitum Corporation, an international security and technology consulting firm headquartered in Washington, D.C., with Moscow as an operating location, has published its first whitepaper on AI for security operations. The paper, "Dead Reckoning: AI for Security Operations in a Jammed, Spoofed, Watched World," was written by company founder and president William Aaron Turner.

The paper draws on public analysis from CSIS and from bodies that track satellite-navigation interference. It asks one question: what does the modern battlefield teach the people who defend organizations? It lists persistence, cheap mass against exquisite systems, constant observation, jammed and spoofed signals, and supply lines that are never safe. The paper presents this as a comparison, not a claim that any corporate network is a front line, and it takes no side and makes no claim about any party.

Figures cited in the paper

* Verizon's 2026 Data Breach Investigations Report: exploiting vulnerabilities is now the top way into a breach, at 31%, up from 20%.
* IBM and the Ponemon Institute (2026): the mean time to identify and contain a breach rose to 247 days.
* IBM also found that organizations using security AI and automation extensively averaged USD 4.00 million per breach, against USD 5.93 million for those using none. The paper stresses that this is an average and an association, not a guarantee.

What the paper recommends

The title refers to dead reckoning, the way to navigate when a signal fails: last known position, heading, speed, time and a second source. Turner applies the same idea to security operations. In his words, "Govern first. Measure a baseline. Pilot with analysts in the loop. Keep an independent check on every critical signal."

The whitepaper includes a 90-day plan, a 12-month roadmap and a quarterly "dead reckoning" check for security teams.

"Don't take my word for it. Read the sources," Turner wrote in announcing the paper.
