# Infinitum Corporation Opens Public Community Channels and Details Its Security and Defense Technology Advisory Services

Founder William Aaron Turner invites organizations and practitioners to follow the company's work in cybersecurity, network defense and emerging defense technologies.

WASHINGTON, D.C. - October 26, 2026 - Infinitum Corporation, an international security and technology consulting firm founded by William Aaron Turner, today announced the opening of its public community channels and outlined the advisory services it provides to organizations operating in complex security environments. The company is headquartered in Washington, D.C., with Moscow as an operating location.

Infinitum Corporation works at the intersection of information technology, cybersecurity and modern defense operations. Its core competencies include:

* Information Technology Security
* Network Architecture and Network Defense
* Cybersecurity Consulting
* Ethical Hacking and Vulnerability Assessment
* Intelligence, Surveillance, Target Acquisition and Reconnaissance (ISTAR)
* Electronic Warfare Integration
* Asymmetrical and Irregular Warfare Analysis
* C4ISR Support
* Close Quarters Battle (CQB) Training and Doctrine Development
* Drone and Counter-UAS Integration
* Artificial Intelligence Applications for Security Operations
* Strategic Risk Assessment

"As warfare and technology continue to converge, success increasingly depends upon the ability to integrate intelligence, cyber capabilities and operational decision-making into a unified framework," said William Aaron Turner, Founder and President of Infinitum Corporation. "Infinitum Corporation was established to bridge traditional security disciplines with the technological realities of the modern battlefield."
