Russian diesel exports to the US: market note on the Putin–Trump fuel deal

• Russia partially lifted its diesel export ban on 10 Oct to implement the Putin–Trump agreements after their 9 Oct call, TASS reports.

Infinitum view: Energy is becoming the first working channel of Russia–US normalization; watch contract signings and November volumes.

Counter-UAS in Europe: utilities seek powers to down drones

• Reuters reports European grid and infrastructure operators want authority to detect, jam or shoot down drones near their sites.
• France passed a law in August letting approved operators do so; its test site has catalogued 17 counter-drone systems so far, per Reuters.
• Austria plans similar powers; Poland has already authorised operator security units to disable drones. Germany is reviewing its rules.
• Reuters notes Moscow has repeatedly denied carrying out hybrid attacks in Europe.
• Liability is the sticking point: who pays if a downed drone causes damage on the ground.

Infinitum view: The hardware is ready; the legal chain of command is not. Expect automated, push-button C-UAS to win the procurement race.

Source: Reuters via Defense News

Russian air defense: MoD reports 581 drones and two Storm Shadow missiles downed in 24 hours

• Per the Russian MoD (via RIA Novosti and Interfax, 26 Oct), air defenses shot down 581 Ukrainian fixed-wing drones, two guided aerial bombs and two UK-made Storm Shadow cruise missiles in a day.
• Of those, the ministry said 290 drones were downed overnight (20:00 on 25 Oct to 09:00 on 26 Oct MSK) over 15 regions, including the Moscow region (NTV).
• A further 37 were intercepted between 08:00 and 20:00 MSK over Belgorod, Bryansk, Kursk, Oryol, the Moscow region and the Black Sea, per the MoD (Kommersant).

Figures are the Russian MoD's own claims.

Infinitum view: Mass one-way drone raids are now a daily volume game, and the side that wins is the one with cheaper interceptors per kill, not just more launchers.

Sources: RIA Novosti | Interfax | NTV | Kommersant
