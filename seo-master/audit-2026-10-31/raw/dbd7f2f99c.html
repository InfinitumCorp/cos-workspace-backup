Infinitum Corporation Expands Global Defense and Technology Advisory Operations
Press Release August 1, 2026
Founder William Aaron Turner Highlights Integrated Approach to Cybersecurity, Defense Technology, and Modern Security Operations
Share This Story:

MOSCOW, RUSSIA, August 01, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Infinitum Corporation, an international security and technology consulting firm founded by William Aaron Turner, today announced the continued expansion of its defense advisory and technical consulting capabilities. Operating at the intersection of information technology, cybersecurity, and modern military operations, the company provides specialized expertise designed to help organizations navigate increasingly complex security environments.

Willian Aaron Turner, Founder and President of Infinitum Corporation, is currently engaged in professional work in Russia, where he focuses on the integration of emerging technologies with contemporary defense and security concepts. His work spans multiple disciplines, combining technical expertise with practical operational planning in support of complex security challenges.

Infinitum Corporation's core competencies include:

Information Technology Security
Network Architecture and Network Defense
Cybersecurity Consulting
Ethical Hacking and Vulnerability Assessment
Intelligence, Surveillance, Target Acquisition, and Reconnaissance (ISTAR)
Electronic Warfare Integration
Asymmetrical and Irregular Warfare Analysis
Command, Control, Communications, Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance (C4ISR) Support
Close Quarters Battle (CQB) Training and Doctrine Development
Emerging Defense Technologies
Drone and Counter-UAS Integration
Artificial Intelligence Applications for Security Operations
Strategic Risk Assessment

"As warfare and technology continue to converge, success increasingly depends upon the ability to integrate intelligence, cyber capabilities, and operational decision-making into a unified framework," said William Aaron Turner. "Infinitum Corporation was established with the objective of bridging traditional security disciplines with the technological realities of the modern battlefield."

The company emphasizes multidisciplinary problem solving, recognizing that modern security challenges rarely exist within a single domain. By combining expertise in cybersecurity, network engineering, intelligence support, and operational planning, Infinitum Corporation develops integrated solutions tailored to complex environments.

Turner has spent years developing technical expertise in cybersecurity, secure communications, network defense, and emerging technologies. His experience informs the company's approach to protecting digital infrastructure while supporting organizations operating in dynamic and rapidly evolving environments.

Looking ahead, Infinitum Corporation intends to expand research into artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, secure communications, electronic warfare, unmanned aerial systems, and advanced network resilience. These initiatives reflect the company's long-term commitment to supporting the evolution of security technology through innovation and practical application.

"Our objective is straightforward," Turner added. "To remain at the forefront of technological innovation while delivering practical security solutions that address today's operational realities and anticipate tomorrow's challenges."

As global security continues to evolve, Infinitum Corporation remains committed to advancing technical excellence, responsible innovation, and collaborative partnerships across government, commercial, and international sectors.

About William Aaron Turner and Infinitum Corporation

Infinitum Corporation is an international technology, security, and strategic consulting firm founded by William Aaron Turner. The company specializes in cybersecurity, information technology, network security, intelligence support, ethical hacking, ISTAR (Intelligence, Surveillance, Target Acquisition, and Reconnaissance), electronic warfare integration, irregular and asymmetric warfare consulting, and modern defense technologies.

Operating at the convergence of technology and security, Infinitum Corporation develops innovative solutions for government agencies, defense organizations, and commercial enterprises facing increasingly complex operational environments. The company's multidisciplinary approach integrates cyber operations, secure communications, artificial intelligence, unmanned systems, intelligence analysis, and infrastructure protection to enhance resilience and decision-making.

Led by Founder and President William Aaron Turner, Infinitum Corporation is committed to advancing practical innovation through technical excellence, strategic insight, and mission-focused execution. Its long-term vision is to help shape the future of security by delivering integrated capabilities that address the evolving challenges of the digital and operational domains.
