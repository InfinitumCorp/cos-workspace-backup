OWNER-SUPPLIED CONCEPT REFERENCE

ORBIS ORB / Structural prototype

The design reference for the interactive mockup: white open frame, olive mock core, and black cradle.

DEVELOPMENT OVERVIEW
Concept / inert prototype

Development concept — proposed functionality and integrations are not a statement of commercial availability.

The owner-supplied ORBIS ORB concept depicts a see-through, soccer-ball-style frame formed from top and bottom hemispheres. An olive mock core and camera enclosure sit inside the frame; a separate black cradle supports the orb for display and fit, form, and integration testing.

The mock core is a visual placeholder. This concept does not establish flight performance, engineering specifications, or commercial availability.

ETHER is a separate free WiFi and mesh communications product.

Design direction & proposed scope

REFERENCE DESIGN

White frame, olive green lynchpin mock core, and black base/cradle ring. The updated 3D visualization follows the supplied concept sheet; internal elements are inert visual placeholders.

NOMINAL PROTOTYPE DIMENSIONS

The supplied concept sheet specifies a 220 mm orb diameter and an 80 mm mock core. These are owner-provided design dimensions, not independently verified measurements.

PROTOTYPE MATERIAL DIRECTION

The supplied sheet describes an FDM structural prototype using PETG or PLA, with Bambu Lab P1S as the intended printer. Printability and assembly claims require validation against the final CAD and physical prototype.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Inert structural prototype for fit, form, integration testing, research, and demonstration. This reference does not establish a flight-ready product or validated operational specifications.
