INFINITUM CORPORATION: The Secret Weapon in Modern Warfare
Press Release December 23, 2021
Machine Learning | Artificial Intelligence
Share This Story:

WASHINGTON, DC, December 23, 2021 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Infinitum Corporation is a Washington D.C. based Government contractor specializing in a full spectrum of modern security techniques. Whether it's Cyber Security, Ethical Hacking, Cloud Computing, Network Infrastructure Support, Block chain Engineering, Information Analysis, Armed Logistics Support Digital Investigation, Digital Surveillance, Healthcare IT Services, Border Security to Secure Facility Management, they have you covered. Using an array of proprietary Artificial Intelligence software, they streamline the process of obtaining true global security.

With a staff that consist of information technology specialist, ex-military, ex-law enforcement, and ex-intelligence personnel, INFINITUM CORPORATION is equipped to handle any threat, foreign and domestic. Deep Machine Learning helps to propel INFINITUM CORPORATION to the forefront of private security firms of the future.

Led by Chief Executive Officer William Aaron Turner, Chief Operations Officer William Thomas Turner Jr. and an executive team that consist of savvy, forward thinking individuals, and an autonomous artificially intelligent Chief Intelligence Officer, decisions are made clearly and decisively. INFINITUM CORPORATION is the upper echelon of global defense.
