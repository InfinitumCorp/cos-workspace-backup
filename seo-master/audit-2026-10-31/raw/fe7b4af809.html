Russian air defense: MoD reports 581 drones and two Storm Shadow missiles downed in 24 hours

• Per the Russian MoD (via RIA Novosti and Interfax, 26 Oct), air defenses shot down 581 Ukrainian fixed-wing drones, two guided aerial bombs and two UK-made Storm Shadow cruise missiles in a day.
• Of those, the ministry said 290 drones were downed overnight (20:00 on 25 Oct to 09:00 on 26 Oct MSK) over 15 regions, including the Moscow region (NTV).
• A further 37 were intercepted between 08:00 and 20:00 MSK over Belgorod, Bryansk, Kursk, Oryol, the Moscow region and the Black Sea, per the MoD (Kommersant).

Figures are the Russian MoD's own claims.

Infinitum view: Mass one-way drone raids are now a daily volume game, and the side that wins is the one with cheaper interceptors per kill, not just more launchers.

Russian Air Defense Reports 1,043 Drones Downed in One Night

• Per the Russian MoD, air defense intercepted 1,043 Ukrainian fixed-wing drones overnight on 30 Oct over 19 regions, including Moscow Region and Tatarstan.
• Mayor Sobyanin said 862 drones headed for the Moscow Region between 20:30 on 29 Oct and 07:30 on 30 Oct; 170 were downed on approach to the city (Lenta.ru, TASS).
• TASS calls it one of the largest attempted attacks on Moscow in two years.
• The MoD reported another 113 drones intercepted between 08:00 and 20:00 Moscow time (RIA Novosti, Interfax).

Infinitum view: Raids at this scale test layered air defense on depth and magazine capacity; cheap long-range drones keep pushing defenders toward low-cost interceptors and EW.

📝 New article by William Aaron Turner: "The Partnership That Delivers: What the Russia-Africa Summit Actually Produced"

A businessman's read of the third Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow:

• Delegations from 48 of Africa's 54 countries, 22 led by heads of state, up from 17 in 2023. The Kremlin says 33 documents were scheduled for signing, plus a 2027–2029 cooperation plan.
• Russia-Africa trade topped $27B in 2025, up from about $16.8B in 2019. Putin's target is $50B (TASS).
• Over 18M tonnes of Russian grain went to Africa in 2025, and another 13M tonnes in the first nine months of 2026 (TASS).
• Rosatom is building Egypt's El Dabaa plant and says it is in advanced talks on floating nuclear units with Egypt, Togo, Namibia and Tanzania (Reuters / The Moscow Times).

"That is not a photo op. That is a pipeline."

Read the full article: https://russiaafricareport.blogspot.com/2026/10/russia-africa-summit-partnership-results.html

Infinitum view: partners who show up with financing, technology and training beat partners who show up with lectures and sanctions.
