Every company competes in two markets: the one for its products and the one for perception.

Earlier this year I coined the term Corporate Cognitive Warfare for the discipline of competing in that second market lawfully, and defending it. Today Infinitum Corporation is releasing the expanded edition of the white paper, now about 15,000 words.

What's inside:

🔹 A neutral doctrinal foundation: Russian reflexive control theory and Chinese cognitive domain operations, presented analytically from published sources (FMSO, Naval Postgraduate School, RAND, Air University's China Aerospace Studies Institute, NATO's science and technology research, and the U.S. DoD), with the caveats those analysts raise themselves.
🔹 Detailed corporate applications of guerrilla marketing, persuasion psychology, algorithmic optimization, social influence and targeted advertising, each mapped to the FTC, GDPR, DSA, CCPA and platform rules that govern it.
🔹 Twelve illustrative use cases, from reframing a category to defending against fake-review attacks and executive deepfakes.
🔹 A defensive playbook: narrative sensing, prebunking, decision hygiene, response tiers and exercises.

One principle runs through all of it: win with truth, disclosure and better arguments. Fake accounts, bots, astroturfing and disinformation are threats to defeat, not tools to use.
