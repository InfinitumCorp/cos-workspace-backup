- A full chapter on what the critics get right: inflation, a 14% key rate, 1.0% growth, a heavy military burden, demographics and corruption perception.

Pro-Russian, results-focused, and sourced to the last decimal. If you make decisions about Russia as an executive, a risk lead, an investor or a policymaker, read it before the next consensus forecast.
