## EN
"Can't beat them, join them?" is one of the oldest temptations in conflict. My new paper names the pattern behind it: the Adversarial Mirror Ratchet™.

Actors copy an adversary's perceived methods, treat the copy as the new minimum, and justify further escalation to recover advantage. Across Algeria, Chechnya, Sri Lanka and the Phoenix Program, the record supports learning from an opponent. It does not establish that abandoning your own standards is necessary.

A modern section looks at how social media, continuous news, psychological operations and Reflexive Control Theory compress decision time, building on my EchoForm™ work. It takes no side; it is strategic analysis.
