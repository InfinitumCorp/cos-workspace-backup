William Aaron Turner Publishes "Can't Beat Them, Join Them?: The Adversarial Mirror Ratchet™"

November 7, 2026 — William Aaron Turner of Infinitum Corporation® has published a strategic analysis paper, "Can't Beat Them, Join Them?: The Adversarial Mirror Ratchet™," which looks at adaptation, terror, intelligence and escalation in the networked age.

The paper introduces the Adversarial Mirror Ratchet™, a term coined by Turner. It describes how actors copy an adversary's perceived methods, treat the copied method as a new minimum, and justify further escalation to recover advantage. Drawing on terrorism scholarship and historical cases including Algeria, Chechnya, Sri Lanka and the Phoenix Program, the paper separates two meanings of "can't beat them, join them": learning from an opponent, and abandoning one's own standards. It argues that history supports the first and does not establish the necessity of the second.

A modern section examines how social media, continuous news cycles, psychological operations and Reflexive Control Theory compress decision time and accelerate escalation, and links this to Turner's earlier EchoForm™ work. The paper takes no side and is offered as strategic analysis.
