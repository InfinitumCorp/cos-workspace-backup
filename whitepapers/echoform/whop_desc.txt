EchoForm™ names how prepared decision grounds return through networked channels as the target's own conclusion—a companion conceptual note to Corporate Cognitive Warfare™. Free download (18 pages) by William Aaron Turner, Founder & President of Infinitum® Corporation, Washington, DC.

Reflexive Control Theory examines how one actor can shape another's decision by transferring the grounds from which the latter reasons. This note proposes EchoForm™ for the modern informational condition around that process: dense networks can return a target's emerging conclusion through social, institutional, and algorithmic channels until repetition resembles independent confirmation. It preserves the classical distinction between directing a decision and shaping its premises, and separates constructed control from ordinary echo chambers, filter bubbles, and general persuasion. A four-step cycle, a concise taxonomy linking the concept to Corporate Cognitive Warfare™, and a neutral account of contemporary mechanisms are included. Emphasis is on detecting source dependence, testing alternative explanations, preserving dissent, and interrupting informational lock-in.
