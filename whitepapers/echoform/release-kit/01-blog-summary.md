EchoForm™ is a term coined by William Aaron Turner for the modern informational condition around Reflexive Control Theory: dense networks can return a target's emerging conclusion through social, institutional, and algorithmic channels until repetition resembles independent confirmation.

This companion conceptual note to *Corporate Cognitive Warfare™* (CCW™) keeps the classical distinction between directing a decision and shaping its premises. It separates constructed control from ordinary echo chambers, filter bubbles, and general persuasion. A four-step cycle—prepared grounds, recursive generation, return through channels, and lock-in—organizes the analysis. A short taxonomy links RCT, EchoForm™, CCW™, and Reflexive Capital Warfare™.

The note stays neutral and defensive. Contemporary military discussion is mechanism-level, not campaign attribution. Corporate illustrations are conceived composites only. Defense—provenance checks, competing hypotheses, premise testing, dissent, and decision reversibility—is central, not an afterthought.
