## Abstract

This whitepaper is a practical guide for mid-size organizations that commission vulnerability assessment, penetration testing and red teaming as defensive services. Using irregular warfare as an analytic lens and the siege as a general image, it argues that every organization is already probed by uninvited testers, so the defender's task is to find and repair weaknesses first. It takes no side, makes no claim about any party and contains no attack instructions. It distinguishes scanning, assessment, penetration testing, red teaming and purple exercises; sets out written authorization, scoping and rules of engagement; compares public methods (NIST SP 800-115, PTES, OWASP, CREST, NCSC, TIBER-EU); and covers evidence-based prioritization, root cause analysis and retesting. Data from Verizon, IBM and Ponemon Institute, CISA and ISC2 frame the problem. The paper offers seven moves, a 90-day plan, a 12-month roadmap and a 30-item self-assessment.
