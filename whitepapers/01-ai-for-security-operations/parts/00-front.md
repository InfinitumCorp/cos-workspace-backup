# About This Paper

This is the first paper in the Infinitum Whitepaper Series. It is written for executives, security leaders and IT leaders at mid-size organizations. By mid-size, I mean organizations large enough to be a worthwhile target and complex enough to have real exposure, but too small to run a large security operations center around the clock with its own research staff.

**How to read it.** If you have ten minutes, read the Executive Summary and the Recommendations. If you have an hour, read it all. The Analysis is where the evidence and the reasoning live. The Recommendations are where the work is. Every statistic in the text carries an inline source tag, such as *(Verizon DBIR, 2026)*, and the full list of sources is in the References section at the end, with links.

**How it is written.** I write in the first person because these are my conclusions. I use the term *irregular warfare lens* throughout as an analytical perspective for defending organizations. It is a way of organizing the problem. It does not describe any personal background, and nothing in this paper is operational or offensive guidance.

**Status.** This is a draft for review. It has not been published.
