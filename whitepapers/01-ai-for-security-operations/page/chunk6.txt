OWASP lists prompt injection as the first risk on its 2025 list (OWASP, 2025). In plain terms, it is when content fed to an AI system alters its behavior in ways its operators did not intend. The content can come from a user directly, or it can be hidden inside material the AI is asked to process, such as an email, a web page, a document or a log entry."},{"t":"p","s":"Why it matters in a SOC. Security AI tools read hostile content by design. That is their job. A triage assistant summarizes a phishing email. An enrichment tool reads a malicious web page. Treat all content processed by AI as untrusted. Limit what the AI tool is allowed to do based on its output: summarizing is low risk; executing changes is high risk. If a tool learns from your own alert history or analyst feedback, an adversary with long-term access could, in principle, influence what it learns.
