The NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework, a voluntary framework organized around four functions: govern, map, measure and manage."},{"t":"p","s":"Asymmetric cost imposition. A situation in which one side spends little to force the other side to spend a lot. Joint doctrine describes irregular warfare as seeking to increase risk and costs to adversaries."},{"t":"p","s":"ATLAS. MITRE's knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques against AI-enabled systems."},{"t":"p","s":"ATT&CK. MITRE's knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques based on real-world observations, used to map detections and find coverage gaps."},{"t":"p","s":"Attribution. Determining who is responsible for an activity. Gray-zone and hybrid activity is often designed to make attribution difficult."},{"t":"p","s":"Authorized testing (ethical hacking). Security testing of systems performed only with explicit, written permission from the system owner, within an agreed scope and rules of engagement, for the purpose of defense."},{"t":"p","s":"Automation bias. The tendency of people to over-trust automated recommendations, especially after the system has been right many times."},{"t":"p","s":"Baseline. A documented picture of normal activity or performance, used to spot deviations and to measure improvement."},{"t":"p","s":"CSF 2.0. The NIST Cybersecurity Framework version 2.0, organized around six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover."},{"t":"p","s":"Data and model poisoning. Manipulation of the data used to train, fine-tune or inform an AI system so that it behaves incorrectly."},{"t":"p","s":"Deception (defensive). Placing decoy assets, credentials or data inside your own environment so that intruders who interact with them reveal themselves."},{"t":"p","s":"Excessive agency. Granting an AI system more ability to act than its task requires."},{"t":"p","s":"Gray zone. The contested space between routine statecraft and open warfare, in which actors pursue objectives while staying below thresholds that would trigger a conventional military response."},{"t":"p","s":"Hybrid threats. Coordinated combinations of military and non-military, covert and overt means, such as disinformation, cyber attacks and economic pressure, used to achieve objectives below the threshold of declared war."},{"t":"p","s":"Irregular warfare (IW). In current U.S. joint doctrine, a form of warfare where states and non-state actors campaign to assure or coerce states or other groups through indirect, non-attributable, or asymmetric activities."},{"t":"p","s":"KEV. CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, a list of vulnerabilities known to have been exploited in the wild."},{"t":"p","s":"Living off the land (LOTL). The use of tools already present on a system, rather than introduced malware, to carry out malicious activity, making it harder to distinguish from legitimate administration."},{"t":"p","s":"Penetration test. An authorized, scoped exercise in which testers attempt to identify and safely demonstrate exploitable weaknesses, under written permission, so they can be fixed."},{"t":"p","s":"Phishing-resistant MFA. Multifactor authentication methods designed to resist credential theft through phishing."},{"t":"p","s":"Pre-positioning. Gaining and maintaining access to a network in advance, for possible later use, rather than for immediate effect."},{"t":"p","s":"Prompt injection. Content supplied to an AI system, directly or hidden inside processed material, that alters its behavior in ways its operators did not intend."},{"t":"p","s":"Proxy. An intermediary actor or piece of infrastructure used to carry out or conceal activity on behalf of another party."},{"t":"p","s":"Resilience. The ability of an organization to absorb disruption, keep essential functions running and recover."},{"t":"p","s":"Shadow AI. The use of AI tools by staff without organizational approval or oversight."},{"t":"p","s":"SOC. Security operations center: the team and capability that monitors, detects, investigates and responds to security events."},{"t":"p","s":"Threshold management.
