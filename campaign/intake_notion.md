# 6. Report and Next Steps Template

## Report outline

1. **Executive summary:** scope, dates, overall risk rating, top 3 issues, in plain language
2. **Scope and method:** assets tested, tools and approach, limitations
3. **Findings:** one entry per issue with the fields below
   - Title and ID
   - **Severity:** Critical / High / Medium / Low / Informational
   - Affected asset
   - Description and impact
   - Evidence (screenshots, requests, steps to reproduce)
4. **Remediation:** prioritized fix list (fix first, fix soon, fix later) with clear steps
5. **Retest offer:** Infinitum offers to retest fixed items to confirm they are resolved
6. **Next steps:** debrief call, follow-up options, contacts

## Confidentiality

All information exchanged during this engagement, including findings and reports, is confidential.
